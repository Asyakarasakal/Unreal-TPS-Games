# Unreal TPS Game

A third-person action-adventure game developed in Unreal Engine 5.

## Overview

The game consists of two chapters connected through a story-driven progression system. The player wakes up inside a dungeon and receives a mysterious mission from an unknown character. Throughout the game, the player uncovers hidden truths while completing objectives and surviving dangerous encounters.

## Features

* Story-driven progression
* Quest Map System
* Interactive dialogue sequence
* Countdown timer system
* Torch and light mechanics
* Hidden objects revealed through torchlight
* Breakable doors
* Physics-based interactions
* Explosive barrels
* Breakable platforms
* Dynamic camera system
* Cinematic ending

## Chapter 1 - The Dungeon

Players must locate three special items and revive a skeleton spirit. The dungeon contains environmental puzzles, hidden objects, breakable doors, and time-based challenges. Candles can be lit to gain additional time before the countdown reaches zero.

## Chapter 2 - The Village

After escaping the dungeon, players obtain a weapon and begin their main mission. This chapter introduces combat mechanics, enemy encounters, environmental interactions, and the Rage Bar system.

## Enemy Types

* Sound-sensitive Skeleton
* Skeleton Warrior
* Golem

Each enemy features unique behaviors and challenges.

## Gameplay Systems

* Rage Bar
* Enemy AI
* Quest Tracking
* Dynamic Lighting
* Environmental Audio
* Death and Respawn System
* Physics Interactions
